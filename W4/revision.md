# Revision Notes: Telephone English (Lecture 25)

## 1. Formal vs. Informal Comparison Matrix

| Context / Action | Formal Phrases (Office, Clients, Professors) | Informal Phrases (Friends, Family) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting a Call** | • *Hello, this is [Name] from [Company].*<br>• *My name is [Name]. I am calling from [Company].* | • *Hi, how is it going?*<br>• *Hi, how are you?*<br>• *What's up?* |
| **Asking for Someone** | • *May I speak to [Name], please?*<br>• *Could I speak with [Name], please?*<br>• *I'd like to speak to [Name], please.* | • *Is [Name] there?*<br>• *Is [Name] around?*<br>• *Can I talk to [Name]?* |
| **Stating the Purpose** | • *I'm calling to ask about...*<br>• *Could you tell me...?* | • *Hey, I just wanted to ask if...*<br>• *Hey, I just wanted to know if...* |
| **Checking Availability** | • *When would be a good time to call?*<br>• *When will he/she be back?*<br>• *Please let me know your best convenient time.* | • *Do you know when she'll be back?* |
| **Ending a Call** | • *No problem. I will try again later.*<br>• *Okay, I will pass on the message.* | • *It was a great conversation. Let us connect regularly.*<br>• *Take care. Let us be in touch.* |

## 2. Taking a Call & Caller Identification

### Professional Call Answering Formula
$$\text{Greeting} + \text{Company Name} + \text{Your Name} + \text{Offer of Help}$$
* **Example:** *"Good afternoon. Airvoice Services, Megha speaking. How may I help you?"*
* **Alternative:** *"Thank you for calling Airvoice Services. This is Megha. What can I do for you today?"*

### Identifying the Caller
* **Unfamiliar Voice (Polite):** *"May/Could I ask who's calling?"* | *"Who's calling, please?"* | *"Who am I speaking to, please?"* | *"Where are you calling from, please?"*
* **Familiar Voice:** *"Hello... Is that [Name]?"*

## 3. Leaving & Taking Messages

* **Caller Initiating:** *"I'd like to leave her a message."* | *"Could you take a message for him?"*
* **Receiver Offering:** *"Would you like me to take a message?"* | *"Hold on, let me grab a pen and a paper."*
* **Key Message Details to Include:**
  1. Caller's name (*"Please tell Padmini that Meera called."*)
  2. Action required (*"Please ask her to call me back."*)
  3. Contact number (*"She can reach me at 7708645321."*)

---

## 4. Putting on Hold, Busy Lines & Second Calls

### Asking Someone to Wait
* **Short Hold:** *"Could you hang on / hold on?"* | *"Would you mind holding, please?"*
* **Apologizing After Hold:** *"Sorry to keep you waiting."*

### When You Are Busy (Callback Phrases)
* *"I am **tied up** at the moment. Can I call you back in an hour's time?"*
* *"If it is not urgent, can I **buzz** you later in the evening?"*
* *"I won't be able to have a long chat as I am driving now."*

### Handling a Second Incoming Call
* *"I am receiving a second call. Could you hold for a second/minute?"*
* *"Please hold on a sec. My boss is **pinging** me."*

## 5. Conference Call Workflow & Phrasal Verbs

1. **Requesting to Add:** *"Can I get Sita with us?"*
2. **Adding Caller:** *"Wait a second, let me **rope in** Sita into this call."*
3. **Connecting Parties:** *"I am having Geetha with me on the other side, I am **patching you in**."*
4. **Audio Check:** *"Geetha and Sita, am I audible to both of you?"*
5. **Dropping One Caller:** *"Geetha, please stay on the line; Sita, you could disconnect. I will call you later."*

## 6. Troubleshooting Call & Network Issues

| Issue Type | Key Expressions to Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Checking Connection** | • *Hello, are you there?* / *Can you hear me?* / *Am I audible?*<br>• *Can you hear me loud and clear?* / *Is my voice too low?* |
| **Audio Distortion** | • *I can hear you **on and off**.* / *...in **bits and pieces**.*<br>• *Your voice is **breaking** / **jarring** (vibrating noise) / **echoing**.*<br>• *I think you got **cut off**.* |
| **Low Battery** | • *My battery is about to **die** / **drain**.*<br>• *I might lose you soon, as I am on 1% battery.* |
| **Dialing / Line Errors** | • *I can't **get through**.* (Single beep / unable to connect)<br>• *The line is **busy / engaged / unobtainable**.* (Multiple beeps or dead line) |
| **Wrong Number** | • *Sorry, I think you have got the wrong number.*<br>• *There is none of that name here.* |

---

## 7. Key Vocabulary & Idioms Quick-Check

* **Tied up:** Busy / occupied with something.
* **Buzz / Ping:** Informal synonyms for calling someone.
* **Rope in:** To persuade or include someone in an activity or call.
* **Patch in:** To connect a third person into an ongoing telephone call.
* **Jarring:** A harsh, vibrating, or unpleasant sound disturbance on the line.
* **Get through:** To succeed in reaching someone by telephone.
* **Cut off:** To be disconnected suddenly during a phone conversation.
